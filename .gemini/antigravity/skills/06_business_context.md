# Skill: Business Context & Strategy

## Metadata
- **ID:** 06_business_context
- **Description:** Market positioning, pricing strategy, and competitive landscape for the Nodeflow IIoT platform.
- **Scope:** Use this skill for brainstorming new features, roadmap prioritization, and ensuring technical decisions support our "Unicorn" trajectory.

---

## 1. Value Proposition: "Shopify for IIoT"
We provide a plug-and-play, AI-powered industrial monitoring platform for SMEs in Singapore and ASEAN who cannot afford custom enterprise solutions.
- **Goal:** Zero-code setup in under 5 minutes.
- **Focus:** Standardized hardware + Powerful Cloud software.

## 2. Target Market & Verticals
1. **Vertical 1 (Current):** Energy Efficiency & Monitoring.
   - High ROI for customers due to rising energy costs.
   - Strong alignment with Singapore government grants (EEG, PSG).
2. **Vertical 2 (Future):** Cold Chain & Food Safety.
3. **Vertical 3 (Future):** Predictive Maintenance for Facilities.

## 3. Pricing Tiers (S$)
- **Starter (S$99/mo):** 10 devices, basic alerts, 30 days data.
- **Professional (S$299/mo):** 50 devices, AI insights, 1 year data.
- **Business (S$699/mo):** 200 devices, full automations, white-label options.
- **Enterprise:** Custom volume + Dedicated support.

## 4. Competitive Strategy
- **VS. ThingsBoard:** We are easier to use, pre-configured for ASEAN hardware, and have integrated AI insights.
- **VS. Generic Dashboards:** We have deep industrial protocol support (Modbus/OPC-UA) and a native "Maintenance" workflow.
- **Unicorn Path:** Scale across ASEAN by leveraging regional hardware partners and government digitalization grants.

## 5. Strategic "Unicorn" Features
- **AI Anomaly Detection:** Automated detection of abnormal energy spikes.
- **Auto-Ticketing:** Connecting machine alarms directly to human work orders.
- **Public Shareable Links:** Allowing SMEs to showcase their "Green" credentials to their customers via public dashboards.
