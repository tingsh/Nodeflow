Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need dedicated hardware to use Novena?

You can use Novena with your own hardware using our open-source edge gateway, or purchase a pre-configured Novena Node for zero-config setup. We support Modbus, MQTT, and Siemens S7 protocols out of the box.

Is there a limit on how much data I can store?

We don't limit storage by gigabytes. Instead, we limit by retention time (7 days, 30 days, or 90 days) based on your plan. All critical alerts are logged indefinitely.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes. Novena is a month-to-month service. You can cancel at any time from your dashboard settings without any cancellation fees.
